TradeMettle Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

TradeMettle is a checklist and rule keeper for intraday traders. It has no account and connects to no broker, and your rules and Days History are stored only on your phone. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, measures how the app is used with Google Analytics for Firebase, and reports crashes with Firebase Crashlytics; Google collects some data for each, as described below.

What stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • your rules: trades per day, losses in a row, break, pause and breath times, the setup rule you write, and preferences;
  • today's session: trades used, losses in a row, open trade time, break timers, and whether you stopped for the day;
  • Days History: for each session, its date, entry and exit times, win, loss, break-even or abandoned outcomes, rule breaks and grade.

This stored data isn't uploaded. The usage events described under Usage analytics do report what happens in the app as it happens, such as a trade's result or why a day ended, but never prices, amounts, profit or loss figures, or the wording of your checklist. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes the stored data.

Backups

If Android backup is on, Android may include your rules and Days History (not the live session) in your device backup, stored in your Google account under Google's terms. The same data can move to a new phone when you transfer your apps. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Ads (Android)

The Android app shows one ad from Google AdMob on the Days History screen. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app details, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with the ad, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostics about the ads software.

This data goes to Google, not to us. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the phone). The iOS app currently shows no ads.

Usage analytics (Android)

The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the app is used, so we can tell which parts help traders and which need work. For that, Google collects:

  • an app instance ID, and your device's advertising ID;
  • device and app details, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • your country or region, derived from your IP address;
  • automatic events, such as first open, session start and app updates;
  • usage events, listed below.

The usage events are:

  • which screen you open: Home, History, Settings or the introduction;
  • finishing the introduction, and whether you picked your own limits;
  • completing the pre-trade checklist, with how many checks it has and whether you changed it;
  • logging a trade entry, with its number in the session and whether it broke a rule;
  • logging a trade's result as a win, a loss or a break-even;
  • a break or pause starting after a result;
  • the day ending, why (the loss limit, every trade used, Done for today, or midnight), and how many trades it had;
  • a rule being broken, and which kind;
  • using undo, resetting the day in Settings, and tapping the break reminder or "Review today in History";
  • opening a session or picking a date in History;
  • changing a rule, the checklist or a preference: which rule changed (not its new setting), how many checks the checklist now has, and whether a preference is now on or off.

Each event is sent with the time it happened, so together the events show how you used the app on a given day. They never include prices, amounts, profit or loss figures, or the wording of your checklist. Read Firebase's privacy information.

Crash reports (Android)

If the Android app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report to Google so the problem can be found and fixed. A report contains:

  • the error and where in the app's code it happened (a stack trace);
  • device and app details, such as model, operating system, available memory and app version;
  • a Crashlytics installation ID, which isn't linked to your name or accounts;
  • the time of the crash.

Crash reports never include your rules, sessions, setup rule or Days History. Google keeps them for 90 days. Read Firebase's privacy information.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads use your data. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • We don't sell your data, and we have no servers that hold it.
  • The app asks for no location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions. It asks to send notifications only when you turn on the reminder for the end of a break. It uses internet access only for ads, usage analytics and crash reports.

Children

TradeMettle is meant for adults who trade, and isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
