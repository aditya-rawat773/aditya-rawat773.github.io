TradeMettle Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

TradeMettle is a checklist and rule keeper for intraday traders. It has no account, connects to no broker, and never sends your trading records to us. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob and counts usage with Google Analytics for Firebase; Google collects some device data for both, as described below.

What stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • your discipline rules (daily bullets, red-streak limit, cooldown and breath times, and preferences);
  • today's live session (bullets used, red streak, open trade time and shield timers);
  • Days History: for each session, its date, entry and exit times, win, loss or abandoned outcomes, rule breaks and grade.

We never receive any of it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes it.

Backups

If Android backup is on, Android may include your rules and Days History (not the live session) in your device backup, stored in your Google account under Google's terms. The same data can move to a new phone when you transfer your apps. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Ads (Android)

The Android app shows one ad from Google AdMob on the Days History screen. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app details, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with the ad, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostics about the ads software.

This data goes to Google, not to us. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the phone). The iOS app currently shows no ads.

Usage analytics (Android)

The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count installs, app opens and sessions, and to see which app versions and devices are in use. For that, Google collects:

  • an app instance ID, and your device's advertising ID;
  • device and app details, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • your country or region, derived from your IP address;
  • automatic events, such as first open, session start and app updates.

Analytics never receives your rules, sessions or Days History. Read Firebase's privacy information.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads use your data. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in, and no crash-reporting tools.
  • We don't sell your data, and we have no servers that hold it.
  • The app asks for no location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions. It uses internet access only for ads and usage analytics.

Children

TradeMettle is meant for adults who trade, and isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write to the developer email shown on TradeMettle's Google Play listing.
